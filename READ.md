# Express Dispatch

Express Dispatch is a courier and logistics tracking platform.

## Core Features

- Public shipment tracking
- Tracking numbers
- Shipment timelines
- Barcode support
- QR-code support
- Live tracking architecture
- Customer dashboard
- Admin operations portal
- Courier management
- Facility management
- Shipment management
- Notifications
- Analytics
- Audit logging
- Role-based access control

## Brand

Express Dispatch uses:

- Brown
- Gold
- White
- Limited black
